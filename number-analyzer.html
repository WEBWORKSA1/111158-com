How the analyzer reads a number

Chinese number symbolism rests on homophones, digits that sound like other words. 8 (bā) sounds like 发 (fā, prosper), 6 like 流 (flow), 9 like 久 (lasting), and 4 like 死 (death). Combinations become phrases: 168 is 一路发 ("prosper all the way"), 518 is 我要发 ("I will prosper") and 1314 is 一生一世 ("a lifetime").

FAQ

How is the prosperity score calculated?

Each digit carries a traditional value (8 prosper, 6 flow, 9 lasting, 4 avoided). Well-known combinations add or subtract points, and endings in 8 and repeating runs earn bonuses.

Why is 4 unlucky?

4 (sì) sounds like 死 (death) in Mandarin and Cantonese, so it is widely avoided in phone numbers, floors and prices. In Teochew culture, 4 can be viewed positively.

Is a high score a guarantee of success?

No. It measures cultural perception. Many customers do value lucky numbers, which is why businesses use them in pricing and branding.
