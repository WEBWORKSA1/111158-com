Decoded

Why 111158 reads like a money mantra

In phone-number Chinese, 1 is read yāo, which sounds like 要 ("want / will"). 5 sounds like 我 ("I") and 8 like 发 ("prosper"). Put together, 111158 becomes 要要要要我发, "I am determined to prosper".

Four ones also echo 11.11, the date that became the world's biggest shopping festival. The number is ambition and commerce in six digits.

Read the way phone numbers are spoken (1 = yāo), 111158 sounds like 要要要要我发: "want, want, want, want, I prosper". In plain English, "I'm determined to get rich".
