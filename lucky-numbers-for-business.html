Lucky numbers for business

Six places where number symbolism moves revenue, and how to apply each one this quarter.

By the 111158 Editorial Team · Updated September 2026

Quick answer Use 8, 6 and 9 and avoid 4 in prices, phone numbers, addresses, product names and launch dates. The payoff is trust and memorability with Chinese-speaking customers. That audience includes the world's largest e-commerce market and large diaspora communities in North America, Southeast Asia and Oceania.

1. Prosperity pricing

Anchor festival bundles at 88, 168, 288, 518 or 888. Test "…8" endings against your current ".99" endings. Never use a 4 ending. Use the Lucky Price Calculator →

2. Phone numbers

A business line with repeating 8s or 6s feels established and successful. Premium numbers are often sold by carriers or at auction. At minimum, avoid 4s and 14/74 sequences. Score your number →

3. Addresses and units

Many buyers and tenants avoid 4th floors and addresses with 4. Units numbered 8, 18, 28 or 88 are often easier to lease. Property marketers highlight them.

4. Names, SKUs and domains

Numeric brand names (think 168, 518, 888) and numeric domains are easy to type without pinyin and easy to say in any dialect. Product codes ending in 8 are a cheap, positive signal.

5. Launch dates and times

Open on the 8th, 18th or 28th, or on a double date (6/6, 8/8, 9/9). Time the go-live for 8:08 or 18:18. Find your best dates →

6. Festival campaigns

Chinese New Year, 520, 618, Qixi, Mid-Autumn, Golden Week, 11.11 and 12.12 are all built-in demand peaks. Plan 6–8 weeks ahead. Open the countdown calendar →
