Customs that matter for businesses

  • Red and gold everything. Red wards off bad luck. Gold signals wealth.
  • Red envelopes (红包). Give them to staff, partners and children. Calculate amounts →
  • Gifting. Oranges and tangerines stand for gold and luck. Gift boxes should come in pairs or 8-based quantities.
  • Reopening day (开工). Pick an auspicious date and hand out start-of-work red envelopes.

Taboos to avoid in campaigns

  • No clocks, which suggest "attending a funeral" (送钟). No pears ("separation") and no sets of 4.
  • Avoid white or black gift wrap.
  • Don't say "broken", "death" or "poor" in greetings or copy.

Lucky foods

Fish (鱼, yú, sounds like "surplus"), dumplings (shaped like silver ingots), niangao (年糕, "higher every year"), longevity noodles and tangyuan (reunion).
