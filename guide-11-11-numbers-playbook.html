The 11.11 Numbers Playbook

By the 111158 Editorial Team · Updated September 2026

Quick answer Singles' Day (11 November) grew from a student joke about "four bare sticks" into the world's largest shopping event, with more than US$150B in yearly sales reported across platforms. Win it with 8-ending price points, pre-sales that open 2–3 weeks early, bundles in lucky quantities, and timed drops at 20:18 or 00:00.

1. Price like a local

Shoppers compare dozens of offers. An ¥88, ¥168 or ¥518 price stands out as "made for the occasion". Avoid 4 anywhere in the final checkout total, including after coupons.

2. Bundle in lucky quantities

Packs of 2, 6 and 8 read as pairs, smoothness and prosperity. Avoid 4-packs in Chinese-facing listings.

3. Own the calendar

  • Mid-October: tease and collect deposits or waitlists
  • Late October: pre-sales open
  • 11 November, 00:00: flash launch
  • 12.12: a second-chance sale for anyone who missed out

4. Countdown psychology

Visible countdowns, like the one above, lift urgency. Pair them with red-envelope coupons (红包券) in lucky denominations: 8, 18 or 88 off.

5. Trademark note

Some platform-specific festival names are registered trademarks in certain jurisdictions. Use descriptive wording such as "Singles' Day" or "11.11 sale" in your own marketing, and check local rules.
