Red envelope etiquette in 60 seconds

  • Use new, crisp notes. Coins and mixed bills look careless.
  • Avoid 4 in any form (40, 400, 440).
  • Even amounts are standard for happy occasions. Odd amounts are traditionally for funerals, in white envelopes.
  • Wedding gifts should at least cover your seat at the banquet.
  • Businesses often hand out 开工利是 (start-of-work red envelopes) on the first working day after New Year.
