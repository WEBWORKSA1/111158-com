Why numeric domains win in China

By the 111158 Editorial Team · Updated September 2026

Quick answer Chinese has thousands of characters and no alphabet, so Latin-letter brand names require pinyin, which many users find awkward to type and remember. Numbers need no translation, read clearly across Mandarin, Cantonese and other dialects, and carry lucky meanings. The result is a deep market for numeric .com names. Reported sales include 55.com at about $2.3M and 114.com at about $2.1M.

Four drivers of value

  • Typing speed: no pinyin input method needed.
  • Cross-dialect: everyone reads digits the same way.
  • Meaning: 8, 6 and 9 add luck; homophone phrases (168, 518) add a message.
  • Scarcity: only 100 two-digit and 1,000 three-digit .com names exist.
