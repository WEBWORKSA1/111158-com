About 111158

要要要要我发: set the intention, then prosper.

111158 is an independent website that turns one of the world's richest traditions of number symbolism into free, practical tools for people who build and sell things.

Numbers carry real commercial weight in Chinese culture. They shape prices, phone numbers, license plates, launch dates and the biggest shopping festivals on earth. Most resources treat this as trivia. We treat it as a business advantage, and we explain it clearly and respectfully.

What we believe

  • Useful first: every page should help you decide something.
  • Respect the culture: meanings are reviewed for accuracy and regional variation.
  • Honest framing: symbolism is about perception, not guaranteed outcomes.
  • Free access: tools stay free, funded by ads, sponsors and supporters.

Editorial policy

Content is written and reviewed by our editorial team and cites public sources where figures are used. Sponsored content is always labelled. Found an error? Tell us and we'll correct it quickly.
