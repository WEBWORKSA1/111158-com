The meaning of 111158

要要要要我发: "want, want, want, want, I prosper." A six-digit ambition mantra.

By the 111158 Editorial Team · Reviewed for cultural accuracy · Updated September 2026

Quick answer Read the way Chinese speakers say phone numbers, 111158 is yāo yāo yāo yāo wǔ bā. 1 (yāo) sounds like 要 "want/will", 5 (wǔ) like 我 "I" and 8 (bā) like 发 "prosper". The whole number reads 要要要要我发, "I really want to prosper". Its core, 158 (要我发, "I will prosper"), is a well-known lucky code.

1. Digit by digit

8bā发 (發)to prosper, to get rich

Why yāo and not yī? When reading phone numbers, room numbers and codes aloud, Chinese speakers say yāo for 1 so it is not confused with 7 (qī). That is why 1 in a number string often carries the sense of 要, "want".

2. Hidden combinations

  • 58: 我发 "I prosper". It's a favourite price point (¥58).
  • 158: 要我发 "I will prosper". It's a popular code in phone numbers and slang.
  • 1111: four ones. This is the date form of Singles' Day (11.11), today the world's largest shopping event.
  • Ends in 8: finishing on prosperity is considered the strongest position.

3. The 11.11 connection

Four ones look like four "bare sticks". That's slang for single people, and it's why 11 November became Singles' Day, which started among university students in the 1990s. Since 2009, e-commerce platforms have turned it into the largest shopping festival on the planet, with combined sales reported at more than US$150 billion a year, above Black Friday and Prime Day combined. The "1111" at the front of 111158 carries that commercial energy.

4. Why lucky numbers are big business

  • A Chengdu phone number with eight 8s sold to an airline for about US$280,000.
  • Hong Kong vanity license plates have sold for millions of dollars at government auctions.
  • The Beijing 2008 Olympics opened at 8:08:08 pm on 8/8/08.
  • Many buildings skip 4th floors. Some luxury towers jump straight to "88".
  • Short numeric .com domains have sold for millions, because numbers need no pinyin input and work across dialects.

5. How to use 111158

For brands, 111158 is a memorable, positive "goal" number. It suits prosperity campaigns, savings challenges, entrepreneurship communities and New Year promotions. Its message is simple and universal: set the intention, then prosper.
