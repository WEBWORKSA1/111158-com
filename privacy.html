Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

1. What we collect

Tools: numbers, dates and other inputs you type into our calculators are processed in your browser and are not sent to us. Forms: when you submit a form (growth plan, newsletter, contest, careers, donation pledge, advertising or contact), we receive the information you provide. This is typically your name, email and the details you enter. Technical data: our host and analytics or advertising partners may process IP addresses, device and browser data, and cookies.

2. How we use it

We use it to respond to requests, deliver the newsletter you opted into, run contests, evaluate applications, process pledges, improve the site, and show advertising. We do not sell personal information.

3. Advertising & cookies

We may use Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. You can change your cookie choice at any time by clearing this site's storage.

4. Service providers

Form submissions are delivered through a third-party form relay service. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Videos are served by YouTube (privacy-enhanced mode where embedded). Each provider processes data under its own privacy policy.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, PIPEDA, PIPL), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data and to object to processing. To make a request, use the contact form (topic "Privacy / data request").

6. Retention & security

We keep submissions only as long as needed for the purpose collected, or as required by law, and we use reasonable safeguards to protect them.

7. Children

This site is not directed to children under 13 (or 16 in the EEA). Contests are open to people aged 18+ only.

8. Changes

We will post updates on this page with a new date.
