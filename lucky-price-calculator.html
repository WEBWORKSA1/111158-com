Why lucky prices work

Prices ending in 8 carry the sound of 发 (prosper). 88 doubles it, 168 reads "prosper all the way" and 518 reads "I will prosper". For Chinese-speaking shoppers, these endings signal a considerate, auspicious offer. That's why restaurant set menus, gift boxes and festival bundles cluster at ¥58, ¥88, ¥128, ¥168 and ¥288.

Rules of thumb

  • Never end on 4 (sounds like "death"), and avoid 14, 74 and 514 inside a price.
  • For gifting products (CNY, Mid-Autumn, weddings), choose even, 8-heavy prices.
  • For bundles, try ¥168 / $168 anchors. They read well in any currency.
  • Pair a lucky price with a lucky launch date for festival campaigns.
