How businesses choose launch dates

Choosing an auspicious date (择日, zé rì) is a long-standing practice for openings, weddings and moves. Numerically, days with 8 are favoured for prosperity and 6 or 9 for smooth, lasting results. Days with 4 are avoided. The most famous example is the Beijing 2008 Olympics, which opened on 8/8/08 at 8:08:08 pm.

Retail openings do best on Fridays and Saturdays, when foot traffic peaks. Deal signings and corporate events suit mid-week. Weddings suit weekends.
