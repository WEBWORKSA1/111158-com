Prosperity pricing

By the 111158 Editorial Team · Updated September 2026

Quick answer Western retail uses "charm prices" (.99) to look cheaper. Chinese retail often uses lucky prices (…8, 88, 168) to feel auspicious. Restaurants commonly price set menus at ¥58 or ¥88, much like $X.99 in the West.

A 14-day A/B test

  • Pick 3–5 SKUs with steady traffic.
  • Variant A keeps the current price. Variant B moves to the nearest lucky price (calculator).
  • Measure conversion rate, average order value and gross margin, not just revenue.
  • Segment by language and region if you can.
